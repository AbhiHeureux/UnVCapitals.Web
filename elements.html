03 Accordion / FAQ

How do I receive awareness updates?
Follow us on social media (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) or join our WhatsApp updates to start receiving regular financial awareness content.
Is the educational content free?
Yes — our awareness content is shared with the goal of promoting financial literacy and accessible knowledge for everyone.
Do you provide personalised financial advice?
No. All content is for general educational and awareness purposes only. Please consult qualified, SEBI-registered professionals before making financial decisions.
How are fees structured?
We operate on a transparent fee-only model — no commissions, no conflicts of interest.
Do you offer international services?
Yes, we serve clients in 30+ countries. Our digital-first approach means we can work with anyone, anywhere.
Can I switch advisors if needed?
Absolutely. Client satisfaction is our priority. If you'd prefer to work with a different advisor on our team, we make that transition seamless.
